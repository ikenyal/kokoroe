# Personas: does the mechanism hold when people vary?

Run on 11/4 (JST), requested 11/5. **This is not a substitute for a real-world test.** It checks how the mechanism tolerates
variation between people.
- **What is right is still decided by the machine:** the frozen hidden kokoroe (`data/hidden`) and the machine judge.
- **What the personas change:** only how the master expresses it (whether and how a remark is said, how ○△× is shown,
  mis-taps) and how the successor asks.
- **Separate from the master-role test** (Kento's own measured session, `docs/master-role-test.md`). The two are not mixed.

**Setup**
- Fictional data: 10 clients, 40 training and 20 held-out scenes. Script: `eval/personas.ts`. Raw results:
  `docs/results/personas-2026-11-04.json` and `docs/results/personas-successors-2026-11-04.json`.
- **Master personas:** one training path each. The same seed for all (101), held-out ×1.
- **Successor personas:** asked against the 教科書どおり master's grown 心得帳.
- **Wording:** long stories, abbreviations and question styles were written by Haiku from the machine's own remark or
  question. They are cached in `eval/personas-texts.json` (committed), so a rerun reuses the same text.
- **Spend:** $9.76 for the first run (limit $10), then $1.43 to rerun the 昔話の師匠 (Kento OK, limit $1.6): **$11.20** in all.

## How to read this
- **One training path per persona.** Two identical runs have ended 10 points apart before (D-024), and the three interim paths
  of the same configuration gave 58–70% (mean 64%).
  - The 教科書どおり path here reached 90%, high for this configuration.
  - Differences of 5–20 points in match between personas are within what a single path can do by itself. Read the match column
    as "did it collapse?", not as a ranking.
- **"Wrongly learned" rules:** an active rule counts if the reading it came from would not have cleared any of the machine's
  actual failures on that draft.
  - The rules it flags read as plausible etiquette (e.g. 「正式な連絡の宛先は窓口の主査に」), and the same 2–3 appear for
    almost every persona, the 教科書どおり master included.
  - So this measure mostly counts rules learned at a scene where the draft actually failed on something else. It is not a sign
    that a persona taught wrong etiquette.
- **The 昔話の師匠 was rerun** (Kento 11/4, $1.43). In the first run 17 of its long remarks came out empty, a generation bug
  (`max_tokens` 400 → 1500, and an empty text now stops the run). The row below is the rerun; every story was generated. It ran on
  the same code as the other rows, before 「どこが違う？」.

## What held, and what did not

**Held**
- **No collapse:** under every master persona except the storyteller, the apprentice still ended at 65–70% match and 85–95% ○+△.
  That is inside the range the same configuration gives with the textbook master on other paths (58–70%). The storyteller is just
  below it (55%, ○+△ 80%; see below).
- **Fewer rules, not wrong ones:** silent and vague masters gave the interpreter less to go on. It wrote fewer rules (17 against
  22), and the "wrongly learned" count did not rise.
- **Mis-taps:** the fat-fingered master slipped twice on a mark (none on a reading). Its rules and "wrongly learned" count are in
  line with the baseline.
- **The successor never made things up** on far questions, however it was asked. Indirect, curt or outsider phrasing cost at most
  one history question.

**Did not hold as well (for Kento to decide whether to fix; nothing in learning was changed)**
1. **Silent and 「なんか違う」 masters:** 「どれでもない」 rose from 31% to 45–52% of the readings shown. With no words, the
   interpreter's readings often missed, and the master had to tap 「どれでもない」. The lesson was then often lost.
   - Possible fix: when there is no remark and the readings miss, offer one more tap, 「どこが違う？」 — 宛先 / 手段 / 時期 /
     裏の一言 / 書式 — so a structural hint replaces words.
   - It would change learning, hence the official measurement. **Kento adopted it (D-057);** see "After 「どこが違う？」" below.
2. **Busy-month master:** the lowest ○+△ (85%). Lenient ○ in busy months taught nothing there, and strict △ (「もう一工夫」) in
   calm months sent the interpreter looking for a difference that was not there.
   - Possible fix (UI only): show 「前回この取引先の似た場面には ○ を付けています」 next to the marks, so the master can see
     their own inconsistency.
   - **Not adopted (Kento, D-058):** a past ○ shown to a master who is going easy may pull them further towards ○. Recorded as a
     known limit.
3. **前職の常識で聞く中途:** the successor's answers held. But the persona text generator failed on 3 of its questions in the first
   run (empty). That was the test's bug, not the product's; fixed and rerun.

4. **昔話の師匠:** the weakest row (55% match, ○+△ 80%, 「どれでもない」 56%).
   - When the machine had no remark, this master tells a story with no point (as designed). The interpreter still reads a lesson
     out of it: e.g. from a story about a factory manager who "looked for the cause with us", it offered
     「不具合が出たら一緒に原因を探す姿勢を…伝える」.
   - So a pointless story misleads, where silence only gives nothing. The master must reject more readings.
   - **Kept as a known limit (Kento 11/4):** with a master who talks at length, the interpreter can make a 心得 out of a story
     with no point. The master's 「どれでもない」 is what stops it.

## After 「どこが違う？」 (D-057, Kento 11/4)

**What changed.** When the master taps 「どれでもない」 on the first readings, the same tap can say where the draft was off: 宛先 /
手段 / 時期 / 裏の一言 / 書式 / その他. It is optional, and it is only a hint for the second readings (see D-057).
- **Simulated master:** it taps the area of the field that would clear its most serious failed check.
- **Skip rates** (assumptions, so that not every master is assumed to answer): 背中で語る 50%, 「なんか違う」 30%, everyone else 20%.

**Rerun:** the same seed (101), one path each, held-out ×1. Raw results: `docs/results/personas-chooser-2026-11-04.json`.
**Spend:** $2.80 for the two target masters, then $1.24 for the 教科書どおり master (Kento OK, limit $4.3): **$4.04**.

**How to read it**
- **Silent master:** fewer lessons lost (52% → 30%) and more rules learned (17 → 20).
- **Vague master:** the share of 「どれでもない」 at the end did not fall (45% → 50%).
  - With an area, its second readings hit 4 of 9.
  - It skipped the area 7 times, and all 7 of those second rounds missed. On this path it was also shown readings more often in
    all (24 against 22).
  - So the hint works when it is given, and the overall rate depends on how often the master gives it.
- **Taps rose** (126 → 136, 129 → 135): more rules learned means more of the optional 「なぜ」 prompts and 確かめ稽古. The chooser
  itself adds no tap: it is the same tap as 「どれでもない」.
- **教科書どおり (the baseline, and the master of the official measurement): one path cannot say "not worse".**
  - **What moved:** match 90% → 70% and ○+△ 95% → 80%, while 「どれでもない」 fell (31% → 25%) and rules rose (22 → 23).
  - **The paths split before the chooser:** the two training logs differ from the very first correction (T04), where the
    interpreter worded the same readings differently. The first 「どこが違う？」 came later (T07). So the gap is mostly the known
    variation between paths (D-024), not the chooser.
  - **Where the chooser was used, it helped:** the second readings hit 3 of 5 with an area. The one flagged rule (K-015,
    「正式な連絡の宛先は窓口の主査とし…」) is the one flagged for almost every persona, and it did not come from a second reading.
  - **In range or not:** 70% match is inside the range of earlier paths of this configuration (58–70%). 80% ○+△ is below their
    90–95%.
  - **Where the answer will come from:** the official measurement on 11/7 (5 training paths, held-out × 3) runs on this version.
- **Single paths:** match differences of 5 points are inside what one path does by itself (see "How to read this"). The
  second-readings table is the more direct measure, and its numbers are small.
